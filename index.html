Meet Dr. Jekyll

A biography of total honesty and nothing else.

Henry Jekyll was born into a distinguished family of means, raised with a deep respect for the law, science, and the importance of keeping certain laboratory doors locked at all times. From an early age, Henry demonstrated an exceptional dualsingular commitment to public service.

He attended the finest institutions, where classmates recall him as "unusually composed," "always present during daylight hours," and "someone whose handwriting never changed. Not once. Absolutely consistent." [1]

After a celebrated career in medicine — during which he authored the landmark paper "On the Separation of Certain Elements of the Human Character for Purely Academic Purposes, I Assure You" — Dr. Jekyll turned his attention to public life. [2]

He has no known associates by the name of Hyde. He has one known associate by the name of Hyde who is him. This has been confirmed. Moving on. [3]

Dr. Jekyll currently resides at a prestigious address in the city's finest quarter, where neighbors describe him as "quiet," "rarely seen after dark," and "the source of occasional unsettling sounds that we have all agreed not to discuss."
