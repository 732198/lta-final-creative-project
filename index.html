Career Timeline

What Actually Happened

  • 1865 Born. A distinguished beginning to a distinguished life. Born. Both of us, though no one knew it yet. I was quieter then.
  • 1883 Graduated top of class. Sole valedictorian. Only one speech given. Graduated. His speech was eighteen minutes of carefully managed self-congratulation. I timed it.
  • 1891 Published groundbreaking research in experimental chemistry. Received the Hargreaves Medal. Finished the compound. The first time he drank it, I walked out of him like a man leaving a building he'd been locked in for thirty years. It was extraordinary.
  • 1894 Took an extended sabbatical for personal research and reflection. This is when I had the city. Three years of it. He tells people he was "unwell." He wasn't unwell. He was losing.
  • 1896 Rejoined public life. Friends noted he seemed refreshed and focused. He regained control. Reformulated the compound. Changed the locks, as it were. I've been waiting since then. Patience is easier when you don't age.
  • 1901 Entered public life. Announced Senate campaign. Has been exactly one person ever since. He ran for Senate thinking visibility would protect him. That if enough people were watching, I couldn't come out. He forgot that I don't mind being watched. He's the one who minds.
